      How this was made

      Every DP topic in AA and AI (SL and AHL) was compared, outcome by outcome, with the MYP 8, MYP 9 and MYP 10 curricula in the Curriculum library (September 2026). A topic is R when the pathway already teaches its main outcomes, P when it teaches prerequisites or an introduction, and N otherwise. SL courses are checked against the Standard pathway (MYP 9 S → MYP 10 S), HL courses against the Extended pathway. DP outcome wording is paraphrased from the IB guides, so check borderline cases against your copy of the guide and discuss them with the department.
