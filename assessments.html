How collecting assessments works

  1. Add your teachers, then click Request assessment. You can send the same request to several teachers at once.
  2. Open a request and click Copy request link, or use Copy reminder in the overview. Send it by email or Teams. The link opens the submission form already filled in.
  3. The teacher fills in the submission form (document link, marks, criteria, self-check) and sends you the .json file it creates.
  4. Click Import submissions and choose the files. They are matched to your requests automatically, then you review, give feedback and approve.

Tip: keep this folder in a shared OneDrive/SharePoint folder so the submission link works for your teachers. Teachers keep the actual papers in Drive/OneDrive and share the link.
